A Warm Welcome to Gyan Niketan

For over two decades, Gyan Niketan has been a second home to the children of Badan Nagar and surrounding villages. We believe every child carries a unique spark — our role is to nurture it with knowledge, discipline and kindness.

Our English-medium curriculum is delivered by teachers who know each student by name. Beyond the classroom, our science labs, computer suites, sports grounds and cultural clubs help students grow into confident, responsible citizens.
